About TikTok Gift Values

TikTok gifts are virtual items viewers can send to creators during livestreams and videos. Each gift has a different coin value, and creators receive a percentage of the final value after TikTok fees.

This TikTok Coins to USD Calculator helps estimate how much TikTok gifts and coins are worth in real money. Values may vary slightly depending on region, taxes, platform fees, and TikTok updates.

How TikTok Coin Conversion Works

TikTok users purchase coins using real money. These coins can then be exchanged for gifts such as Roses, Lions, Galaxies, Universes, and other popular TikTok livestream gifts.

Creators receive diamonds from gifts, which can later be converted into earnings. TikTok keeps a portion of the revenue as platform fees.
